Method
Recommendations are stored append-only, so this measures what the system said at the time rather than a reconstruction of what it would say now. Each is graded once its evaluation horizon elapses — 180 days for fundamental long, 120 for special situations, 90 for bearish, 45 for technical swing — against the benchmark return over the same window, so a call is credited with the excess return it produced rather than with a rising market. Short and SELL calls are judged on the decline they anticipated.
