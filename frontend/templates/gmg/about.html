About {{ brand.company }}

{{ brand.company }} builds research technology for the Egyptian Exchange. {{ brand.product }} is our subscription platform for investors who want the analysis an institution would run, with the honesty about data that retail tools rarely offer.

What we believe

Most investment tools fail their users in the same way: they present a number without saying where it came from, how old it is, or whether it was measured at all. A price with no timestamp, a ratio computed from a statement that was never filed, a "fair value" with hidden assumptions — each of these is worse than no number, because it invites a decision built on nothing.

So {{ brand.product }} is built the other way round. Every price carries its source and its delay. Every score shows its components and the weight each carried. Every missing figure says N/A — data unavailable rather than defaulting to zero. Every valuation shows the assumptions you can change. Where our AI writes commentary, it is separated from the figures and constrained to what the data supports.
