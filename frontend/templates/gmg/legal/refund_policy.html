1. Try before you pay

Every account starts with a {{ plan.trial_days }}-day free trial with full access and no payment details required. We would rather you decide during the trial than pay for something that does not suit you.

If you are dissatisfied with your first paid subscription period, contact us within 14 days of that payment and we will refund it in full. You do not need to give a reason. Access ends when the refund is issued.

3. Renewals

Renewal payments are not routinely refunded, because you can cancel at any time before a renewal and keep access to the end of the period you paid for. We will still consider a refund where:

  • The Service was unavailable for a prolonged period within that billing period;
  • You were charged after cancelling;
  • You were charged more than once for the same period;
  • A fault on our side materially prevented you from using what you paid for.

4. What is not refundable

  • Investment losses. A refund covers the subscription fee only. {{ brand.company }} is never responsible for the outcome of your investment decisions.
  • Periods where you had access but chose not to use it.
  • Accounts terminated for breach of the Terms of Service.

5. How to request a refund

Write to us through the contact page from the email address on the account, telling us which payment you mean. We reply within two business days and, where a refund is due, issue it to the original payment method within 14 days of approval. The time it then takes to appear depends on your bank or payment provider.
