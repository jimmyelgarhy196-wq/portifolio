1. Who controls your data

{{ brand.company }} is the controller of the personal data described here. You can reach us through our contact page.

2. What we collect

Information you give us

  • Your email address and password (stored only as an Argon2id hash — we never store or can recover your plaintext password);
  • Your name, if you provide it;
  • Your marketing preference;
  • Content you create: watchlists, portfolio entries, saved screens, alerts, and messages you send us.

Information collected automatically

  • Sign-in records: time, IP address and browser user-agent, kept so you can see your own sessions and so we can detect unauthorised access;
  • Failed sign-in attempts, kept to enforce rate limits and lockouts;
  • An audit log of account and subscription events;
  • A log of emails we sent you and whether they succeeded.

What we do not collect

  • We do not store card numbers. Where a payment provider is connected, card details are handled by that provider and never reach our servers.
  • We do not use third-party advertising or analytics trackers on the application.
  • We do not sell personal data to anyone, ever.

4. Who we share it with

Only the providers needed to run the Service, and only what they need:

  • The email provider that delivers our messages;
  • The payment provider, once one is connected, which handles payment data directly;
  • Our hosting provider, which stores the database;
  • Authorities, where we are legally required to disclose.

5. How long we keep it

  • Account data: while your account exists, then deleted or anonymised within 90 days of a deletion request;
  • Sign-in and audit records: up to 24 months, for security;
  • Payment and invoice records: as long as tax and accounting law requires;
  • Email logs: 12 months.

6. Your rights

You can ask us to:

  • Give you a copy of the personal data we hold about you;
  • Correct data that is wrong — you can edit most of it yourself on your account page;
  • Delete your account and personal data, subject to records we must keep by law;
  • Stop sending marketing email — either from your account page or by replying to us;
  • Object to or restrict certain processing.

Your account page shows the sign-in records, audit events and emails we hold for you, and lets you sign out every session. To request deletion, contact us.

7. Security

  • Passwords are hashed with Argon2id, a memory-hard algorithm, and are never logged;
  • Session tokens are stored only as SHA-256 digests, so a database copy does not yield usable sessions;
  • Session cookies are HTTP-only and, in production, Secure and SameSite;
  • Changing your password invalidates every existing session;
  • Access to paid features is enforced on the server, not in your browser.

No system is perfectly secure. If we discover a breach affecting your personal data, we will notify you and the relevant authority as required.

8. International transfers

Our providers may process data outside Egypt. Where they do, we rely on the provider's contractual data-protection commitments.

9. Children

The Service is not for anyone under 18, and we do not knowingly collect their data.

10. Changes

We will post any change here and, where it materially affects you, tell you by email.
