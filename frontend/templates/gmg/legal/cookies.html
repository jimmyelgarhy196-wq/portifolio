1. The short version

{{ brand.product }} uses one cookie, and only when you are signed in. We use no advertising cookies, no third-party analytics, and no cross-site tracking of any kind. There is no consent banner because there is nothing to consent to beyond the cookie that keeps you signed in.

2. The cookie we set

NamePurposeLifeType
{{ settings.session_cookie }} Keeps you signed in. Contains a signed session identifier — no personal data, and the matching secret is stored only as a hash on our server. Up to {{ settings.session_days }} days, or until you sign out Strictly necessary

It is set HttpOnly so scripts cannot read it, and SameSite=Lax so it is not sent from other sites. In production it is also set Secure, so it travels only over HTTPS.

3. Browser storage

The application may use your browser's sessionStorage to remember which tab you last opened on a page. That stays on your device, is never sent to us, and disappears when you close the tab.

4. What we do not use

  • No Google Analytics or comparable analytics service;
  • No advertising, retargeting or social-media pixels;
  • No fingerprinting;
  • No third-party fonts, scripts or charting services that would see your activity — charts are drawn locally in your browser.

5. Controlling cookies

You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings. Blocking our session cookie means you cannot stay signed in, so the application will not work.

6. Changes

If we ever add a cookie, we will update this page before doing so and, where the law requires consent, ask for it first.
