1. The plan

{{ brand.product }} is offered as a single subscription plan:

  • {{ plan.name }} — EGP {{ plan.price_egp | fmt_num(0) }} per {{ plan.interval }};
  • A {{ plan.trial_days }}-day free trial, which does not require payment details;
  • Full access to every feature listed on the pricing page.

2. Free trial

Your trial begins when you create your account and runs for {{ plan.trial_days }} days. It gives you the same access as a paid subscription. When it ends, paid features stop being available until you subscribe. We do not charge you automatically at the end of a trial: starting a subscription is always an action you take.

3. Billing

Subscriptions are billed for one {{ plan.interval }} at a time, in Egyptian pounds. The period runs from the date payment is confirmed. If you renew before your period ends, the new period is added to the end of the existing one, so no paid time is lost.

Where no payment gateway is connected to this deployment, requesting a subscription records your intent only — no card is charged — and an administrator activates your access once payment has been confirmed by the agreed method. {{ brand.company }} does not operate a simulated checkout.

4. Cancelling

  • Cancel at any time from your account page. No notice period and no cancellation fee.
  • Your access continues to the end of the period you have already paid for.
  • The subscription then simply does not renew.
  • You can resume before the period ends and keep the same billing date.

5. What happens when access ends

Your account, watchlists, portfolio entries, saved screens and alerts are retained. Paid features become unavailable, and access is re-enabled the moment you subscribe again. You can request deletion of your data at any time.

6. Price changes

We may change the price. Any change is notified by email at least 30 days before it takes effect and applies from your next renewal, never mid-period. If you do not accept a new price, cancel before it takes effect.

7. Fair use

A subscription is for one person. Sharing credentials, reselling access, or bulk-extracting data is a breach of the Terms of Service and may result in suspension.

8. Suspension for non-payment

If a renewal payment fails we will tell you and mark the subscription past due. Access continues for any period already paid. If payment is not resolved, the subscription expires at the end of that period.

9. What the subscription does not include

It does not include investment advice, portfolio management, trade execution, custody of money or securities, or any guaranteed outcome. See the Investment Disclaimer.
