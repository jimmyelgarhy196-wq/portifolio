Where our data comes from

Every number on {{ brand.product }} carries its source, its timestamp and any delay. This page states the whole chain in one place, including what is not connected.

What we do not do. We do not scrape TradingView, and we make no claim that TradingView provides an unrestricted real-time market-data API. We do not present generated figures as live prices. Where a provider is unavailable, the platform says so and shows “N/A — data unavailable” rather than filling the gap.

How we handle missing data

  • A missing figure renders as “N/A — data unavailable”, never as zero.
  • A score whose inputs are too sparse is withheld, not defaulted.
  • A ratio whose denominator is zero is unavailable, never infinity.
  • A rating is withheld when score coverage falls below the threshold we require.
  • An alert is skipped, with the reason shown, when the value it needs is unavailable.
  • Demonstration data is labelled everywhere it appears and never fires an alert.
