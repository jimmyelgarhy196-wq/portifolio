How this chart is built

The chart is rendered in your browser from daily bars stored by GMG. Indicators (SMA, Bollinger, RSI, MACD) are computed from those same closes using the standard formulas — nothing is fetched from a third party while you browse, and no chart vendor receives your activity.

Where a bar is missing, the line breaks rather than interpolating a value that was never observed.
